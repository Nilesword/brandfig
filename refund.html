Refund Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

We want you to be happy with Brandfig Pro. Try the free version first to see if it fits your workflow.

14-day refunds

If Pro isn't right for you, email us within 14 days of purchase and we'll refund you in full. No long explanation needed, though we'd love to know what we could do better.

How to ask

Email CONTACT_EMAIL with your order number or the email you used to buy. Refunds are issued by Dodo Payments to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days.

After a refund

The refunded license key is deactivated and Pro features stop working. The free features keep working, and everything you already created in your files stays yours.

After 14 days

We don't normally refund after 14 days, but if something is broken and we can't fix it, get in touch and we'll make it right.
