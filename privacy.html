Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Brandfig is built to keep your work private. This policy explains the little data involved.

Your designs stay in Figma

Brandfig runs inside Figma on your computer. It reads the layers you select only to draw guides and pages in the same file. Your designs, files and layer contents are never uploaded anywhere.

Settings

Your plugin settings (for example your chosen colors and options) are saved with Figma's plugin storage on your own device, so they're remembered next time. We can't see them.

License checks (Pro)

When you enter a Pro license key, Brandfig sends the key and a device label to Dodo Payments to confirm it is valid and to count activations. It re-checks occasionally. Nothing else is sent.

Purchases

When you buy Pro, Dodo Payments collects the details needed for the purchase (such as your name, email, billing country and payment details) as merchant of record. We receive your email and order details so we can deliver your license and support you. We never see your full card details.

Analytics and tracking

The plugin has no analytics, tracking or ads. This website uses no cookies of its own; fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, at any time. Payment records may need to be kept by the merchant of record for legal and tax reasons.
