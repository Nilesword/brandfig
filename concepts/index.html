Questions, answered.

What goes into a logo presentation?

A cover, the brief, three words and references. Then, for each concept: an opener, the logo, versions, construction, colour, type, stationery, digital and in use. It closes with the concepts side by side and next steps.

How many concepts can I present?

You can add up to six. The templates are designed for one to four in a deck.

Can I edit the pages?

Yes. Every page is made of normal Figma layers, so you can change the words, images and layout.

What if a concept isn’t finished?

Pages that would be empty are skipped. Build again when you have more and the deck updates.

What happens after the client picks one?

Take the chosen direction into brand guidelines, then export the logo files.
