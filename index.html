FAQ

Questions

How do I activate Pro?

After you buy, your license key arrives by email. Open Brandfig in Figma, choose “Enter license key” and paste it. That's it.

Is it a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time payment for a lifetime license, including future updates.

How many computers can I use it on?

One license can be activated on up to 3 devices for the same person. Teams need one license per designer.

Who handles the payment?

Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record. They handle checkout, taxes and receipts; we never see your card details.

Can I get a refund?

No. All sales are final, so please try the free version first to make sure Brandfig fits your workflow. See the refund policy for the few exceptions.

Does Brandfig upload my designs?

No. Everything runs inside Figma on your computer. The only thing sent anywhere is your license key, to check it's valid. See the privacy policy.
