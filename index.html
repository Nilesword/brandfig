FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it AI?

No. Construction is geometry: Brandfig reads your logo’s anchor points and curves and draws from them. Decks and guides are templates filled with your own logos, colours and fonts.

How does the free trial work?

Every tool is unlocked for 3 days. You add a card at checkout but nothing is charged until day 3; cancel before then and you won’t be charged at all.

How does the subscription work?

Pro is billed monthly ($7) or yearly ($56, saving 33%) and renews automatically. Cancel anytime from the link in your receipt email; Pro keeps working until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

How do I activate it?

Your license key arrives by email. Open Brandfig in Figma, paste the key and press Activate.

How many computers can I use?

One key works on two computers. Teams need one license per designer.

Does my work leave Figma?

No. Everything runs inside Figma on your computer. The only thing it checks online is your license. See the privacy policy.

Can I get a refund?

Payments aren’t refunded, except duplicate charges or where the law requires it. Try it free for 3 days first. See the refund policy.

Is it coming to Illustrator?

Yes, the Illustrator version is in the works.
